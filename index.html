How to read this score — honestly

This is a keyword-and-evidence guide, not a real ATS and not a hiring decision. Real applicant tracking systems differ by company, and a human still makes the final call. Use the score to find gaps and weak wording — never add skills you don't actually have just to raise it. A resume that scores 75 with true, specific evidence beats a stuffed one at 95 in any real interview.
